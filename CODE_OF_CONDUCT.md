## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent while verifying claims, respect privacy, and
keep criticism on the work and the decisions. Harassment, threats, doxxing,
discrimination, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.

## One thing this repository asks for specifically

This project exists to let a stranger verify bytes they did not build. That only
works if a report of the form "the digest does not match" is welcomed rather than
argued with. Bring the mismatch: the version you checked, the value you computed,
and where you got the file. Nobody needs to be persuasive to be taken seriously
here, and a report that turns out to be a local mistake costs nothing — the
alternative is a reader who noticed something and said nothing.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate measures.
Reports will be handled discreetly, subject to legal and safety needs.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles without
incorporating external contact details.
